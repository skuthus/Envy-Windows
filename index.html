Trash

            
            

              The whole Trash folder is swept into the Recycle Bin this often,
              counting from the last time it was emptied — checked when Envy
              starts and hourly while it runs.
            

            

              
              
            

          


          

            
Note list

            
            
            
            
            
            
            

              For a note in a subfolder: a dot in the folder's colour, the
              folder's name as a chip, or nothing. Needs “Show items in
              subfolders”.
            

            
            

              A note's folder dot or name chip sits just after the title by
              default. Turn this off to move it before the title. The Inbox
              mark always stays on the left.
            

            
            
            

              Everything you write begins as a fleeting note, and filing it
              into The Index becomes a deliberate act — including notes created
              by following a [[link]] that doesn't exist yet. Notes
              made from a template are unaffected, since those arrive with
              structure you chose deliberately.
            

            
            
            

              Whole-vault totals, to the right of the word and character
              counts. The folder count appears only when subfolders are shown.
            

          


          

            
Editor

            
            
            
            
            

              A bare URL renders as a compact pill of just its domain. Purely
              visual and entirely offline — the note still holds the full URL,
              and it shows in full the moment your cursor enters it.
